I'm a third-year PhD Student in Computer Science at Washington State University under the supervision of Professor <a href="https://htnghia87.github.io/">Nghia Hoang</a>. I graduated from Hanoi University of Science and Technology with a bachelor in Applied Mathematics and Informatics. During my undergrad, I was fortunate to be an AI research resident at FPT Software in Hanoi, Vietnam.

 My work spans probabilistic frameworks for model merging, continual adaptation, flow-based models and uncertainty quantification. I am passionate about building scalable, theoretically sound machine learning systems that bridge statistical rigor with practical deployment. I am currently open to research internship opportunities in machine learning, artificial intelligence, and related fields.
